1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday September 15, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 4:00 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This meeting's most consequential item is a consent-calendar vote to adopt resolutions of local support for two Bridgeway corridor improvement projects — one covering Gate 6 Road to Harbor Drive and the other Spring Street to Napa Street — authorizing staff to pursue Regional Measure 3 grant funding for bikeway, roadway, and pedestrian upgrades (3.H). The Council will also hear a Marin County presentation on El Niño king tide preparedness (1.A) and take up a business item on the Turney Street Dock condition assessment and future concept direction (5.B). Other notable items include a second-reading sign ordinance amendment (3.D) and the city's response to a Civil Grand Jury report on MCE/Marin Clean Energy governance (3.E).

2. Topics of Interest

🚲 Cycling

Item 3.H — Resolutions of Local Support for Bridgeway Improvement Projects & RM3 Grant Applications The Council is asked to adopt (consent-calendar vote) two resolutions of local support: one for the Bikeway/Roadway/Pedestrian Improvements from Gate 6 Road to Harbor Drive and another for the Bridgeway Improvement Project from Spring Street to Napa Street. Both resolutions authorize staff to apply for Regional Measure 3 (RM3) Safe Routes to Transit and Bay Trails grant funding. The accompanying Complete Streets Checklist signals that the projects are designed to serve all modes, including dedicated bicycle facilities along the Bridgeway corridor — a primary cycling route connecting the Sausalito waterfront, the ferry terminal, and the Mill Valley–Sausalito multi-use path.

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

Item 3.H — Resolutions of Local Support for Bridgeway Improvement Projects & RM3 Grant Applications The same item also directly addresses pedestrian improvements. The project title explicitly includes "Pedestrian Improvements," and the Complete Streets Checklist evaluates sidewalk, crosswalk, and accessibility enhancements along Bridgeway. The Spring-to-Napa segment runs through downtown Sausalito where pedestrian volumes are highest. Grant funding through RM3's Safe Routes to Transit program targets safe, walkable connections to the Sausalito ferry terminal.
