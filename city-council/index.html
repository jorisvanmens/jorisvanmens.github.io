October 06, 2026 Meeting Agenda Summary

📄 Download full agenda PDF

1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday October 6, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 3:00 PM · Special Meeting 3:30 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This meeting centers on a contentious housing appeal: a public hearing on the appeal of Planning Commission Resolution 2025-07 regarding a proposed housing development at 605-613 Bridgeway Boulevard filed under SB 330 (1.A). Other notable items include additional funding for the Caledonia Street Restriping Project (4.B), approval of an interim city manager agreement (5.B), a discussion on flood mitigation around Gate 5 Road tied to the Governor's state of emergency (5.D), and a review of a proposed public mural at the Excelsior Stairs (5.C).

2. Topics of Interest

🚲 Cycling

  • Item 4.B – Caledonia Street Restriping Project funding and streetscape design: The Council is asked to appropriate $160,193.60 from General Fund Reserves to fund construction of the Caledonia Street Restriping Project and to support Amendment 1 of SWA's professional services agreement for streetscape beautification design development. Restriping projects often affect bike lane configurations and multi-modal access. Action: Consent calendar vote (approval).

Any modifications to the stairway environment could tangentially relate to pedestrian wayfinding and accessibility. Action: Discussion and direction to staff (no formal vote expected).

🏠 Housing

  • Item 1.A – Public Hearing: Appeal of Planning Commission Resolution 2025-07 for 605-613 Bridgeway Boulevard (Project ID 2024-00014): This is the most significant housing item on the agenda. The applicant (Willy's LLC) has appealed the Planning Commission's decision upholding administrative determinations on a proposed housing development filed under SB 330 (Housing Crisis Act) and the State Density Bonus Law. The project requires Design Review, a Tentative Map, and a Certificate of Appropriateness. The Council is also asked to approve a response to a Challenged Conduct Notice from the applicant. The applicant has submitted a request for continuance. Action: Public hearing and vote.
