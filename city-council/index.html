🏠 Housing

Item 4.A – Public Hearing: Appeal of Planning Commission Resolution 2025-07 re 605-613 Bridgeway Boulevard Housing Development (Project ID: 2024-00014)

The Council will hold a public hearing on the applicant's (Willy's LLC) appeal of Planning Commission Resolution 2025-07, which upheld city staff's administrative determinations regarding a proposed housing development on Bridgeway Boulevard. The project involves design review, a tentative map, and a certificate of appropriateness, and was filed under SB 330 (Housing Accountability Act) with a State Density Bonus Law request. The Council is also asked to approve the City's response to a "Challenged Conduct Notice" filed by the applicant. This is a public hearing with a vote expected. The extensive attachment list — 27 documents including inconsistency determinations, historic resource analyses, site plans, and legal correspondence — signals significant legal complexity and community interest around this project.
