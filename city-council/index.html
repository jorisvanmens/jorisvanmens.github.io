1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday September 1, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 3:30 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This meeting features significant closed-session items around the City Manager position and potential litigation, alongside a packed regular session. The most consequential open-session items are the evaluation of an Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) to fund infrastructure and sustainable development in the Marinship area (5.A), a drainage management study for the Gate 5 area (5.B), and a public hearing on updates to the city's sign ordinance (4.A). The consent calendar includes a notable contract amendment for the Coloma Street Sidewalk Project (3.E) and completion of the Dunphy Park Phase 2 improvements (3.D).

2. Topics of Interest

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

  • Item 3.E — Coloma Street Sidewalk Project: Fourth Contract Amendment with BKF Engineers ($9,300) This consent calendar item authorizes an additional $9,300 for engineering services on the Coloma Street Sidewalk Project, a pedestrian infrastructure improvement. The Council is asked to approve via consent vote.

🏠 Housing

  • Item 5.A — Evaluation of Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) for the Marinship The Council will evaluate the benefit and feasibility of forming an EIFD to support infrastructure investment and sustainable development in the Marinship area. While not a housing item per se, EIFDs can fund affordable housing and infrastructure that supports housing development — the Marinship is a key area identified in Sausalito's planning for future growth. The Council is asked to discuss and provide direction to staff.
