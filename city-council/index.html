November 17, 2026 Meeting Agenda Summary

📄 Download full agenda PDF

1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday November 17, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 4:00 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This agenda is heavy on infrastructure and resilience, with multiple items addressing slope repairs (3.B, 3.C), flood preparedness (3.F), drainage improvements (3.H, 3.M), and the realignment of Willow Creek around Willow Creek School (3.N). Housing-related items are also prominent, including adoption of a historic district defining-characteristics list that will govern housing development review (3.D) and a public hearing on ADU zoning amendments (4.A). A new bike path authorization (3.E) and a holiday parking program (5.A) round out a packed consent calendar and business agenda.

2. Topics of Interest

🚲 Cycling

3.E – Authorize Additional Contingency for Coloma Street Sidewalk Project ($90,000) to Construct a New Bike Path from Tomales Street to Coloma Street - Action requested: Consent Calendar vote to authorize additional contingency spending from the existing project budget. - This item converts or adds a bike path component to what was originally scoped as a sidewalk project, creating a new cycling connection between Tomales Street and Coloma Street. - Links: - Staff Report

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

3.E – Coloma Street Sidewalk Project / New Bike Path - This item also has a pedestrian dimension: the underlying project is a sidewalk project, and the new bike path will share the corridor, improving multi-modal access in the area. - Action requested: Consent Calendar vote. - Links: - Staff Report

3.G – Authorization to Proceed with Installation of Wayfinding Signage in Downtown Business District - Wayfinding signage improves pedestrian navigation and safety in the downtown core. - Action requested: Consent Calendar vote. - Links: - Staff Report listed as "Pending" in the agenda; not yet available.

3.I – Sausalito Police Department Crime and Traffic Report, Q3/YTD 2026 - Includes traffic enforcement data relevant to pedestrian and road-user safety. - Action requested: Receive and file (informational). - Links: - Staff Report

🏠 Housing

3.D – Resolution Adopting a Defining Characteristics List for Buildings Within the Certified Historic District (for Housing Development Projects under Title 10A) - Establishes the specific architectural and design characteristics that will be used to evaluate housing development proposals inside the historic district, per the Objective Design and Development Standards. This directly shapes what housing can look like in one of the city's most sensitive areas. - Action requested: Consent Calendar vote to adopt resolution. - Links: - Staff Report - Attachment 1 – Resolution - Attachment 2 – Defining Characteristics List

4.A – Public Hearing: Amendments to Sausalito Municipal Code Title 10 (Zoning) Section 10.44.080 – Accessory Dwelling Units - Proposes changes to the city's ADU regulations. This is the introduction/first reading of the ordinance amendments, with a waiver of the first reading requested. - Action requested: Public hearing; introduction by title and waiver of first reading (a second reading/adoption vote would follow at a subsequent meeting). - Links: - Staff Report listed as "Pending" in the agenda; not yet available. - California HCD ADU Resources
