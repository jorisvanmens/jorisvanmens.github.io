1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday September 1, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 3:30 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This meeting is heavy on infrastructure and long-range planning. The Council will evaluate whether to form an Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) to fund improvements in the Marinship area (5.A), review a drainage management study for the Gate 5 area (5.B), and hold a public hearing on updates to the city's sign ordinance (4.A). Closed session items focus on City Manager employment matters and real property negotiations for Cass Giddley Marina (C2, C3, C5). The consent calendar includes a sidewalk project contract amendment (3.E) and completion of the Dunphy Park Phase 2 project (3.D).

2. Topics of Interest

🚶 Pedestrian Safety

  • 3.E – Coloma Street Sidewalk Project Contract Amendment — The Council is asked to authorize (consent calendar vote) a fourth contract amendment with BKF Engineers for $9,300 for the Coloma Street Sidewalk Project. This is a pedestrian infrastructure project that involves ongoing engineering services. The amendment brings total contract costs higher to continue design/engineering work on a new sidewalk.

🏠 Housing

  • 5.A – Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) for the Marinship — The Council will evaluate (discussion/direction to staff) the benefit and feasibility of forming an EIFD to support infrastructure investment and "sustainable development" in the Marinship. While framed as an infrastructure financing tool, EIFDs can capture tax increment to fund housing-supportive infrastructure and facilitate development — making this directly relevant to future housing potential in the Marinship area. The accompanying land economics study and strategic plan references underscore the development dimensions.
