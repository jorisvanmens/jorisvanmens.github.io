1. Meeting Overview

Tuesday September 1, 2026 · Special Meeting (Closed Session) 3:30 PM · Regular Meeting 5:00 PM

This meeting features a packed closed session covering anticipated litigation, City Manager employment, labor negotiations, and marina lease negotiations (C1–C4). On the public side, the two headline items are an evaluation of forming an Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) in the Marinship to fund infrastructure and sustainable development (5.A) and a drainage management study for the Gate 5 area (5.B). The consent calendar includes a notable sidewalk project contract amendment (3.E) and capital project management authorization (3.F), while a public hearing addresses a sign ordinance update (4.A).

🏠 Housing

  • Item 5.A – Evaluation of Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District (EIFD) Formation in the Marinship While primarily a financing and infrastructure tool, EIFDs under California law can fund "sustainable development" including affordable housing. The staff report evaluates whether an EIFD in the Marinship area could support infrastructure investment and development, which has direct implications for housing potential in that district. The Kosmont land economics study attached examines development feasibility.
  • Action requested: Discussion/direction to staff (business item)
