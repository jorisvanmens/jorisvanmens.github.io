💬 Public Comments

Respect the Election. Respect the Residents (Marinship / General Plan)

18 public comments were submitted. All commenters urged the City Council to give newly elected representatives Jill Hoffman and Nastassya Saad a voice, direct the Planning Department to complete the merger of the Marinship Specific Plan with the General Plan within 60 days (including allowing makers to sell wares in up to 15% of their space), and opposed creating another task force or politically appointed committee. The sentiment was unanimously in one direction, with many using identical or near-identical language and invoking the recent election results and defeat of Measure X as justification.
