💬 Public Comments

Appeal of Planning Commission Decision – 605-613 Bridgeway (Agenda Item 1A)

18 public comments in support of the appeal/project, 2 opposed, and 1 urging more affordable units. The overwhelming majority of commenters supported the Waterstreet housing project and urged the Council to grant the appeal, citing the site's transit-rich downtown location, the need for housing (including affordable units), the City's Housing Element commitments, and compliance with state housing law. Supporters included housing advocacy organizations (YIMBY Law, California Housing Defense Fund, Housing Action Coalition, MEHC), individual residents emphasizing downtown revitalization and the misleading nature of opposition imagery, and the applicant Linda Fotsch herself. Those opposed—Patricia Libby and Peter Bostock—argued the project is too dense, too tall, and out of character for the historic district, with Bostock supporting the City's conduct and the staff recommendation to deny the appeal. John Gavin and Stacy Nimmo supported the project but urged the Council to require more affordable units and ensure equitable geographic distribution of affordable housing throughout Sausalito.

Art in Public Places – "Viña del Mar Stairs" Mural (Agenda Item 5d)

  • Ron Albert urged the Council to approve the Excelsior Stairs mural proposal, sharing photos of similar colorful staircase artwork in Noto, Sicily as an example of how it enlivens a historic district.

Caledonia Street Restriping / Parklet Removal

  • Virginia Erwin requested removal of the COVID-era parklet on Caledonia Street between Johnson and Pine Streets to restore two-way traffic and parking for the neighborhood.
