💬 Public Comments

Public Hearing on Appeal of Planning Commission Resolution (605-613 Bridgeway / Waterstreet Project)

The agenda item drew approximately 200+ written public comments. The overwhelming majority of commenters oppose the project as proposed. Opposition commenters consistently urge the Council to deny the appeal, uphold staff and Planning Commission findings, protect the Historic District, require full environmental review of traffic/noise/water quality impacts, and ensure any affordable housing is equitably distributed with family-sized units. A smaller number of commenters—including Linda Fotsch (the applicant), housing advocacy organizations (YIMBY Law, Housing Action Coalition, California Housing Defense Fund), and several residents—support the appeal, arguing the City must honor its Housing Element density commitments, comply with state housing law, and approve the project to address Sausalito's housing shortage and revitalize downtown.
